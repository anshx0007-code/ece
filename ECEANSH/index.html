ILLNESS

Illness policies are rules and procedures that are followed by Childcare centre to help protect children, families and educators from the spread of infections. These policies help educators to better understand the signs of illness in children and to respond accordingly.

Three important considerations when managing illness in childcare are: Symptoms of illness, Exclusion from Childcare, Reporting Requirements. These policies are important as it help protect and keep children safe.

Illness related
policies, forms and routines.

Immunization records contain the information regarding the vaccinations that child has received. In Ontario, it is manadatory for Childcare centres to ensure that children are immunized as per the directions of local Ministry before admission and afterwards. There are some exceptions for certain religious and medical reasons (Ontario, 2015, s.35).
These records are important because they helps educators to understand the Immunization of individual child and provide the respond accordingly when communicable disease occurs. In classrooms, educators can follow this requirement by keeping the immunization records in the individual files of children and asking families to provide updates when needed.
